### The start of the walking simulator project, it is for my own development as a developer and also something i can build and work on on my own time and when it is ready add to my cv or portfolio. That's it for now, more details coming later as progress continues.

- I got started on the set up for the player and the camera, i tested the game physics and boundaries with test walls,
steps and jump boxes. After some changes everyhting work fine and i continued on the design of the world

- The main playing area was made to look like a mossy green ground along with evergreen trees and a path through them.
(the testing 3D objects were moved to the side for further testing of the games features)

- I had a bit of a bug trying to make the fog through the forest and after several tests and trials i found the error
in the bloom of the sample scene. The value was too high and everything apart from the ground in front of the players FOV
was completely blown out. I removed the bloom alltogether as it wasnt needed and commited the work up untill now cleanly.

- Next in my agenda is to make the trees and grass more detailed and have a wind effect if possible, before trying again with the
fog and the obtainable items.
